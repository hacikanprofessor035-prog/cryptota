Bollinger Bands Explained — Volatility & Squeeze Trading

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum oscillator that measures how fast and how much a crypto price has moved. It is one of the most popular indicators for spotting overbought and oversold conditions.

What Bollinger Bands measure

Bollinger Bands are a moving average (usually 20 period) with two bands at ±2 standard deviations. They expand when volatility rises and contract when it falls.

The squeeze

A squeeze — bands pinched tightly together — means low volatility and often precedes a sharp move. The direction of the breakout sets the trade. Not every breakout continues, so wait for a close outside the band.

Mean reversion

Price tagging the upper or lower band can act as a reversion signal back to the middle line. This works best in ranges and fails in strong trends, where price can 'walk the band'.

Combining with volume

A squeeze breakout on rising volume is far more trustworthy than one on flat volume. CryptoTA shows volume directly under the price chart.
