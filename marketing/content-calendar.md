## Week 1 — Momentum basics

**Mon — What RSI actually measures**
RSI doesn't measure price. It measures the *ratio* of average gains to average losses over N candles.

That's why RSI = 80 on a coin that's up 300% isn't "wrong" — it just means the move has been one-directional for a while.

What kills traders is treating 70/30 as a button. In strong trends RSI stays extreme for days.

**The real signal: divergence.** Price prints a higher high, RSI prints a lower high → buyers are still buying, but with less force.

Try it on live charts (RSI pane is one click):
https://cryptota.duckdns.org/#/BTCUSDT/4h

**Wed — MACD in one paragraph**
MACD line = 12 EMA − 26 EMA. Signal line = 9 EMA of that.
The histogram = the gap between them.

Crossovers are the obvious trade. But the *histogram shrinking* is the earlier signal — momentum peaks before price does.

**Fri — The BB squeeze**
Bollinger Bands are a 20 SMA ± 2 standard deviations. They widen with volatility and pinch when it's low.

A tight squeeze = the market is coiled. Breakout direction usually sets the next move — but wait for a close outside the band, not just a wick.

Squeeze + rising volume = the highest-probability setup of the three.

## Week 2 — Trend & structure

**Mon — Why traders lose money in "ranges"**
A market ranges about 70% of the time and trends 30%. Most retail strategy is built for the 30%.

Before trading a breakout, check: is price actually compressing, or is it just chopping with no structure?

The heatmap helps here — relative strength across 60 pairs tells you where the trend *already is*:
https://cryptota.duckdns.org/#/BTCUSDT/4h

**Wed — The 20/50/200 stack**
In a healthy uptrend: 20 EMA > 50 EMA > 200 EMA, and price respects the 20 as support.

A 50/200 crossover is the "golden cross" — famous, slow, and usually late. By the time it fires, a chunk of the move is gone.

Faster traders use the touch of a rising 20 EMA as the entry and the 50 as the last line of defense.

**Fri — Multi-timeframe without the noise**
The classic mistake: you see a clean uptrend on 15m, but 4h and daily are in a downtrend. Your 15m signal is a bounce inside a bear market.

Check 3–5 timeframes before committing. The confluence panel does this in one screen:
https://cryptota.duckdns.org/#/BTCUSDT/4h

## Week 3 — Risk ( this one converts )

**Mon — Position sizing is the actual edge**
Two traders, same setup. One risks 2%, one risks 20%.

The 20% trader needs a 25% win just to recover one loss. Six losses in a row — statistically normal over a few hundred trades — puts them down 74%.

A risk calculator makes this mechanical:
https://cryptota.duckdns.org/#/BTCUSDT/4h

**Wed — R:R is a trap if you ignore win rate**
A 3:1 strategy that wins 25% of the time breaks even. A 1.5:1 strategy that wins 60% of the time prints money.

Backtest to find *your* ratio — don't copy someone else's.

**Fri — Where to actually place a stop**
"Illogical" stops (round numbers, just below the low everyone sees) get hunted.

Logical stops: below the structure that invalidates your thesis. If you're buying a bounce off the 50 EMA, the stop goes below the EMA — not an arbitrary percent.
